  • Offline billing — create bills from locally stored items without depending on connectivity.
  • Item catalog — maintain your item list and prices ready for quick counter selection.
  • Live bill totals — build bills line by line with automatic totals, taxes, and payment mode options.
  • Bluetooth thermal printing — print receipts on supported Bluetooth thermal printers, where supported.

Mobile Printing

Bluetooth & Wi-Fi thermal printing

Print customer receipts directly to portable 58mm or 80mm Bluetooth ESC/POS printers, or send print jobs across your counter to a Wi-Fi receipt printer.

  • Standard ESC/POS receipt generation
  • Bluetooth direct pairing from Android settings
  • Local LAN Wi-Fi printer connectivity
Hardware protocols vary by printer manufacturer. We recommend testing your thermal printer model before deployment.
