What it does

Everything essential, in your pocket

The screens your counter needs — billing, items, bill preview, printing, backups and licensing.

BillingItemsBill preview PrinterBackup / restoreLicense
  • Offline billing — create bills from locally stored items without depending on connectivity.
  • Item management — maintain your item list with prices ready for quick selection.
  • Bill creation — build bills line by line with live totals as you go.
  • Bluetooth thermal printing — print receipts on supported Bluetooth thermal printers, where supported.
  • Wi-Fi thermal printing — print to supported network thermal printers over Wi-Fi, where supported.
  • Backup export — export a backup file of your billing data whenever you choose.
  • Backup import & restore — import a backup file to restore your items and billing data.
  • License activation — activate with your annual license key and check for updates in-app.
