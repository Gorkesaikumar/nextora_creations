Nextora POS/Privacy

Privacy Policy.

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what information the Nextora POS website and the Nextora POS applications handle. Nextora POS is a product of Nextora Creations ("Nextora", "we").

Website

This website is designed to work without accounts. We do not require sign-in, and we do not sell personal information.

  • Contact and pricing forms: when you submit a form, the details you enter (name, email, phone, business details) are sent to Nextora so we can respond. We use them only to handle your request.
  • Analytics: we use Google Analytics to understand aggregate usage of this website (pages viewed, approximate location, device type). This does not identify individual users to us and does not cover the applications themselves.
  • Downloads: download links point to files hosted by Nextora. We do not add trackers to downloads.

The applications

Nextora POS Desktop and Nextora POS Android are designed around local data storage for core billing records.

  • Local data: your items, bills and related billing records are stored on your device in the application's local database. We do not receive copies of your billing data.
  • Backups: backups are files you export and control. They stay wherever you put them.

Connectivity in the applications

  • License activation and renewal: the app contacts Nextora's licensing service to validate your key. This transmits the license key and device identifiers needed for validation.
  • Software updates: the update check contacts Nextora's update service to learn whether a newer version is available.

Daily billing, item management, bill generation and receipt printing are designed to work without an internet connection.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect or store your customers' personal data on our servers.
  • We do not embed advertising or third-party ad trackers in the applications.
  • We do not sell information to anyone.

Data security

Because billing data is stored locally, you control it. We recommend regular backups and device security (screen locks, disk encryption) as good practice. No system can guarantee absolute security, and we make no such claim.

Your choices

  • You can opt out of Google Analytics for this website using your browser settings or Google's opt-out tools.
  • To request deletion of contact details you have shared with us, email us and we will delete them.
