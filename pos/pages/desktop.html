Hardware Integration

ESC/POS Thermal Receipt Printing

Connect your counter thermal printer via USB or serial COM port. Nextora Mini POS generates fast, clean ESC/POS receipts with itemized totals, taxes, and customizable business headers.

  • Supports standard 58mm & 80mm thermal receipt formats
  • Direct USB and virtual COM port communication
  • Fast test print utility included in settings
Printer compatibility can vary by manufacturer driver. We recommend testing your model before commercial rollout.
