Kitchen Operations

Live KDS: Instant wireless orders over local Wi-Fi

Nextora POS Desktop includes a built-in LAN server that streams orders directly to any screen in the kitchen over your local Wi-Fi router. Kitchen display tablets connect in seconds using a secure 6-digit code.

  • Operates 100% locally — internet outages never disrupt order transmission
  • Real-time status cards: Pending, Preparing, Ready, and Served
  • Works on any browser-equipped tablet, iPad, or monitor connected to local Wi-Fi
  • Exclusively included with Nextora POS Desktop
