Nextora POS/How It Works

Three steps to your
first bill.

Install, activate and start billing — designed to be running the same day.

01

Install Nextora POS

Choose Windows or Android and install from the official download source. Desktop uses a Windows installer; Android installs from the official APK.

02

Activate your license

Enter the activation key supplied by Nextora in License settings. Activation validates once over the internet — after that, billing works online or off.

03

Start billing

Set up your items and supported printer, then begin billing. Daily billing runs on local data with no internet required.

Licensing

Simple annual licensing.

One license per device, renewed yearly. Your data stays on your device through renewals.

Request Pricing
01

Purchase / request a license

Contact Nextora for an annual license for each device you want to activate.

02

Receive your activation key

We issue a license key for the product and platform you purchased.

03

Activate the device

Enter the key in License settings. Internet is required for activation only.

04

License stays valid

Billing continues normally for the license period — online or offline.

05

Renew annually

Renew before expiry to keep receiving updates and continued support.

What needs connectivity: license activation, software updates and downloads. What doesn't: daily billing, item management, bill generation and receipt printing.
