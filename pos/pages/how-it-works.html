Nextora POS/How It Works

From installation to first bill
in minutes.

Built for restaurant simplicity: install locally, activate your annual key, connect your hardware, and run full-speed offline billing.

01

Install Nextora POS

Download the Windows installer for your billing counter PC, or install the Android mobile app for handheld and table service.

02

Activate your 365-day license

Enter the annual key provided by Nextora. Activation authenticates once over the internet; afterward, all daily operations work 100% offline.

03

Configure menu & hardware

Add menu items, categories, and tax rates. Connect optional thermal receipt printers and pair Live KDS kitchen screens over your local Wi-Fi router.

04

Start billing & monitoring

Generate sequential bills, stream kitchen orders in real time, auto-deduct recipe stock, and monitor sales on Nextora Owner Cloud Analytics.

Annual Licensing

Simple per-device subscriptions.

One transparent annual license per device (Desktop at ₹7,999/yr, Mobile at ₹5,999/yr; thermal printing add-on +₹2,000/yr). No forced bundles or lock-in. Your data stays securely on your device throughout renewals.

Select your edition

02

Receive your license key

We issue a dedicated 365-day license key for your specific activated device.

03

One-time online activation

Enter the key in Settings. After brief validation, the system operates completely offline.

04

Run full-speed operations

Create bills, stream orders to KDS, track stock, and review reports all year round.

05

Seamless annual renewal

Renew before expiry to maintain uninterrupted billing and receive software enhancements.

What needs connectivity: initial license activation, software updates, and optional synchronization to Nextora Owner Cloud Analytics. What doesn't: daily billing, item lookup, bill creation, local KDS wireless kitchen communication, and receipt printing.
