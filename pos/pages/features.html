Nextora POS/Features

Everything restaurant billing needs.
Zero internet dependence.

Built for high-volume restaurants, cafes, and food counters — fast billing, kitchen orders, recipe inventory, and owner cloud analytics.

Offline-first SQLite billing

Runs on local databases. Create bills, apply discounts, calculate GST, and record payment tenders without depending on an active internet connection.

Live Kitchen Display (KDS)

Built-in LAN web server in Nextora POS Desktop streams live order cards to kitchen screens over local Wi-Fi router. 100% offline kitchen coordination.

Kitchen Order Tickets (KOT)

Generate non-priced kitchen preparation tickets with customer special instructions and track live order status from Pending to Served.

Recipe & stock inventory

Map menu dishes to raw ingredients with automatic unit conversions. Quantities deduct automatically on bill creation; track wastage and COGS.

Owner Cloud Analytics

Remote browser portal at owner.nextoracreations.co.in. Sync sales, bills, and hourly trends whenever the counter PC is online.

Thermal receipt printing

Optional add-on (+₹2,000/yr). High-speed ESC/POS receipt printing via USB/Serial on Desktop and Bluetooth/Wi-Fi on Mobile.

Local backups you control

One-click export of complete database snapshots to local storage or external USB drives. Restore safely when upgrading computers.

Transparent annual licensing

Simple 365-day per-device license (Desktop ₹7,999/yr, Mobile ₹5,999/yr). No forced bundles, no vendor lock-in, and full data ownership.
